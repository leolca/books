Preface:

Refusing to go to school is, for many children, a natural step in their path toward growth.  
This refusal often comes in disguise — with traces of truth, seasoned by imagination.  
It’s important to listen to what the child has to say and understand that,  
unable to fully express their feelings, they turn to the justifications that come first to mind.

In this book, we follow Popcorn on his journey of reluctance and discovery.  
Each day, his creative mind invents a new excuse to avoid going to school.  
And each day, reality shows that school can be fun and welcoming —  
and that while his excuses may seem true to him, they are often woven with fantasy.

A child’s imagination is powerful — and sometimes, it serves as a shield.  
With lightness and humor, this story invites adults and little ones  
to face this moment of transition with empathy, listening, and care.

Because, in the end, the hardest part isn’t arriving at school…  
it’s wanting to leave.
